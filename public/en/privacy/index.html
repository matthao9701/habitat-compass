Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

1. Overview & controller

NomadMatch ("the site") is a browser-based decision-support tool for settling abroad. There are no user accounts and no server-side user database — everything that relates to you lives only on your own device.

Data controller: the NomadMatch operator (entity details to be added before official launch). Contact: privacy@nomadmatch.app (placeholder, to be replaced with the real mailbox).

2. What data we process

The site collects and uploads no personal data. Everything you produce while using it is stored only in your browser's localStorage (key prefix nomadmatch.v1):

  • Quiz drafts & answers (draft / proDraft) — destination preferences, budget, questionnaire answers
  • Results & latest history (history / proHistory) — match scores and report data
  • Favorite cities (favorites), compare session and archives (compare / archives)
  • Hard constraints & passport choice (hardConstraints / passport) — budget cap, visa floor, safety threshold
  • Interface language (lang), anonymous funnel counters (funnel) — stage counts only, no identity data
  • Pro unlock state and local order log (proUnlocked / orders) — demo-purchase records, see Terms

All of it is entered by you or derived from your input. It contains no name, e-mail, phone number or precise location.

3. What we do NOT do

No tracking cookies — the site sets no cookies and reads no third-party cookies.

No third-party analytics or ads — no Google Analytics, no ad networks, no social plugins; nothing is sent to anyone.

No AI processing — matching runs entirely in your browser; your answers never reach any AI model or server.

4. Purpose & legal basis

Purpose: only the service you request — keeping quiz progress, generating reports, remembering your language setting (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: processing necessary for the performance of the service).

The site sets no cookies and does not access terminal equipment for tracking. The local storage described above is strictly necessary to provide the service you explicitly requested (cf. the exemption logic of Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). The site therefore shows no consent banner.

5. Storage location & international transfers

All data stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, so there is no international data transfer and no server-side breach surface.

6. Retention

Local data is kept until you erase it — via your browser's "clear site data", or the "Erase all my data" button below.

7. Your rights (GDPR Art. 15–22)

Under the GDPR you have: the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), the right to object (Art. 21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

All data lives on your device, so you can exercise every right instantly and completely:

  • Access / portability: browser dev tools (Application → Local Storage) let you view and export everything
  • Rectification / erasure / restriction / objection: removing the storage keys is the whole act — the button below or "clear site data" does it
  • Need help? E-mail privacy@nomadmatch.app — we reply within 30 days

8. Children (Art. 8)

The service is not offered to anyone under 16. If you are under 16, please do not use the site.

9. Data breach (Art. 33/34)

With no server-side storage there is ordinarily no server breach risk. Should a security incident ever affect your data, we will publish a prominent notice on this page within 72 hours of becoming aware, and notify affected users individually where possible.

10. EEA/UK supplement & California (CCPA/CPRA)

EEA/UK: this policy constitutes the full Art. 13 GDPR information disclosure; processing happens locally on your device, with no processors and no automated decision-making.

California: the site does not sell or share any personal information — data never leaves your device, so "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" is satisfied by design; California residents also enjoy the rights to know, to delete and to non-discrimination.

11. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with an updated date; major changes are announced on the home page.
