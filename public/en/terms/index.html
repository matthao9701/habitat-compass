1. Service description

Habitat Compass provides decision support for digital nomads, freelancers and remote workers settling abroad: it scores the 200 cities in its library against your stated preferences and public data snapshots, and generates a report.

The lite assessment (32 questions), the in-depth Standard edition and city comparison are all free of charge. No account is required.
